Q1 · What data, and why

Pre-training (8T consumed). Gemma-class peers train ~14T; we close the gap with OPUS-style dynamic selection on the general pools (V4 measured up to 6× effective tokens; we claim a conservative 2–3×) — never on Indic, which the V4 selector provably undervalued.

D1 Web foundation34% · 2.7TFineWeb-Edu/DCLM-grade filtered web — language & world knowledge.
D2 Web diverse + India-English14% · 1.1TIncl. 0.45T Indian-perspective English: newspaper archives, court judgments, Parliament/PIB records, Indian historiography. The Indian worldview mostly lives in English written in India — collect it deliberately.

Three structural rules: a 10% always-on batch share for Indic + India-English, exempt from the quality selector (V4's 8% channel, widened — two protected objectives: language and worldview); a 15% anneal reserve of top-decile math/code/documentary-register text set aside on day one for the LR-cooldown (OLMo-2: GSM 24→67 from a tiny reserve); and a staged curriculum — web-heavy at the small seed stages, code/STEM/Indic-heavy at 40B, shares fixed per stage by ablation.

Post-training. SFT ~1M pairs (30% Indic native-authored — translated instructions teach translationese; 25% code w/ execution; 15% agentic multi-turn; 15% reasoning-tagged math/science; 15% general+safety). Preference ~400K, incl. a 100K India corpus by Indian annotators — register & honorifics per language, neutrality across Indian parties, Indian legal-ethical frames: this is where "India-first" is actually installed; pre-training supplies knowledge, preferences supply stance. RLVR ~80K verifiable prompts (30K math-checked, 25K unit-tested code, 15K sandboxed agentic tasks, 10K Indic with FLORES-anchored + script-correctness verifiers) — verifiers get their own test suite, since a broken verifier teaches the wrong behaviour.

Q2 · Cleaning, for these objectives

  1. Extract with a rendering-based extractor (not naive HTML strip); NFC-normalize; per-paragraph script ID; Hinglish kept first-class.
  2. Quality-filter script-aware: FineWeb-Edu-style classifier for English; a per-language fastText classifier for each Indic language, seeded from ~5K native-speaker-labeled docs and grown by iterative mining. An English-tuned filter is never applied to Indic — that is precisely how V4's selector erased it. Indian-English registers ("prepone", "do the needful") whitelisted.
  3. Dedup per snapshot (exact + MinHash) — global dedup removed ~58% and scored worse in FineWeb; cross-dataset dedup for code corpora.
  4. Decontaminate against every target benchmark and our own held-out sets: source exclusion + exact + n-gram + embedding similarity, with canary GUIDs planted to detect leaks after the fact.
  5. Provenance & license at collection time (permissive / attribution / restricted buckets) — one unknown-provenance source flags the whole blend; Sangraha's CC-BY obligations honored.

Every step is validated by 140M–1B proxy runs that only need to rank recipes, not predict final scores. A corpus change without an ablation is an opinion.

Q4 · Fertility targets → vocabulary size

Fertility (tokens per FLORES-200 word; lower = better, 1.0 ideal) is the real data budget: at V4-era fertility, "500B Hindi tokens" was ~135B words. Targets, LP-allocated per measured deficit:

Dravidian targets sit higher because agglutinative words are longer — the LP equalizes per-character cost, not per-word optics. V4-era baselines for scale: Hindi 3.7×, Bengali 4.4×, Tamil 7.1×, Telugu 8.2×. Agentic gets the tightest non-English target because agentic traces are our longest contexts — low fertility there buys the most usable context window.

Vocab arithmetic. Start from BrahmicTokenizer-131K's retrofit method (prune out-of-scope scripts, LP-allocate slots to measured deficits — its 2,372 Indic slots already gave Odia 4.3× compression). Needed beyond 131K: ~45K Indic subword slots (≈3–4K per language to pull Dravidian under 1.9), ~6K math/LaTeX (\frac, \sum as single tokens), ~4K agentic/markup (JSON keys, schema punctuation), ~2K code-mixing; ~10K reclaimed by pruning. ≈178K → round to 196,608 = 3×2¹⁶.
Why it's affordable: Kronecker embeddings make the input side ~50M params regardless of vocab; only the softmax head scales — 196,608 × 5,120 ≈ 1.0B params, 2.5% of the model. Why not 262K: <2% marginal fertility gain and a fatter undertrained tail (LP constraint: no slot below ~50K expected occurrences in 8T). Why not 131K: Dravidian fertility stays ≥2.0, forfeiting ~7% of the effective corpus on the flagship objective. Weighted mixture fertility lands at ≈1.22 — 8T tokens ≈ 6.5T words, ~9% more content for the same compute than the V4-era 1.33.
